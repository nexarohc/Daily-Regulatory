What it does

Primary sources, continuously polled

Every item in the feed comes from an authority's own publication feed and links straight back to the original notice. Nothing is rewritten, and nothing is invented.

Worldwide coverage

Authority feeds across North & South America, Europe, Asia, Oceania, the Middle East, Africa and the international bodies (WHO, ICH, EDQM).

Continuous polling

Each feed is re-checked on a fixed interval using conditional requests, so new notices surface quickly without hammering the authority's servers.

Triage at a glance

Items are auto-tagged by type and severity — recalls, safety alerts, approvals, guidance, enforcement — so the urgent ones stand out. Always labelled as automatic.

Registered access only

Server-side sessions, hashed credentials and CSRF protection. The feed API refuses every unauthenticated request.

Feed health, visible

Every source reports when it was last reached. A moved or broken endpoint shows up in the admin panel instead of silently going quiet.

Bookmarks & filters

Filter by region, authority, category, severity or free text, and keep a personal shortlist of notices worth tracking.
